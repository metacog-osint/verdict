The ledger in full

How it works

  • Pre-registration. A forecast enters the ledger before its outcome can be known; the public commit is the timestamp. A forecast registered after the deciding news was public is void.
  • Words and numbers. Each probability is stored as a number and named with the US intelligence community's likelihood terms (ICD 203). Confidence is rated separately and never folded into the odds. Language key.
  • Scoring. The Brier score: the squared gap between the probability and what happened (0 is perfect, 0.25 is a coin toss on every question), with its breakdown into calibration and resolution as the record grows.
  • Nothing is edited. A correction is a new ledger line that names the one it replaces; both stay public. Method.
